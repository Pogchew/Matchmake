## Service Overview

Matchmake is an esports operations tool for school, collegiate, and amateur teams. The service helps teams:

- Create organizations and teams.
- Manage rosters and team metadata.
- Post, request, accept, decline, cancel, complete, and review scrims.
- Coordinate scrim schedules.
- Use active scrim chat.
- Review match results, player rows, and team statistics.
- Use optional scoreboard screenshot extraction to help populate match review data.
- Use optional calendar feeds or Discord calendar digests when configured.

## School And Student Use

Matchmake is intended for school-authorized esports activities and related team operations. School administrators, coaches, and organization owners are responsible for:

- Approving use of Matchmake for their program.
- Deciding which students, coaches, and staff may use Matchmake.
- Managing team rosters, public team information, scrim listings, chat expectations, and match review content.
- Handling school notices, consents, and parent/guardian communications required by school policy or law.
- Reviewing whether roster names, gamer tags, participation in esports, or similar fields are directory information under the school's own FERPA notice before making those fields public.

Matchmake is not a substitute for school supervision. Schools and coaches remain responsible for supervising student activity, team conduct, eligibility decisions, event rules, and disciplinary decisions.

## Eligibility And Under-13 Use

Matchmake is intended for high school, collegiate, and amateur esports teams. For the MOSEF / high-school pilot, Matchmake should not allow self-service use by children under 13.

Under-13 use is allowed only when:

- The school or district has specifically authorized that use in writing.
- The required COPPA notice and consent path has been completed before collection from the under-13 user.
- The use is limited to the school-authorized esports team coordination and match review context.
- Matchmake and the school have agreed how parent/guardian review, correction, deletion, and revocation requests will be handled.

The operational decision and under-13 response workflow are documented in `docs/under-13-coppa-handling.md`.

Users who are not authorized by their school, team, parent/guardian where required, or organization owner may not use Matchmake.

## Accounts And Security

Users must provide accurate account information and keep login credentials secure. Account sharing is not allowed unless Matchmake and the school have expressly approved a shared administrative account workflow.

Schools, organization owners, and coaches are responsible for promptly removing access for users who should no longer participate in their program.

Users must notify `[support contact email]` or their school/team administrator if they suspect unauthorized access, account compromise, or misuse.

## Organization And Team Administration

Organization owners and authorized coaches/admins may create and manage organizations, teams, rosters, scrim listings, calendar links, match reviews, and related content.

They are responsible for keeping organization and team information accurate, school-appropriate, and authorized for the audience where it appears.

If an organization creates a live calendar subscription link, anyone with that link may be able to view the calendar feed until the link is rotated or disabled. Organization owners are responsible for controlling distribution of calendar links.

## Scrim Listings, Requests, And Chat

Users may use Matchmake to post and request scrims in good faith. Users may not intentionally post misleading availability, impersonate another school/team, falsify team ranking information, spam requests, or use scrim workflows to harass other teams.

Scrim chat is for coordination between active scrim participants. Users may not use chat for harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, sexual content, doxxing, cheating coordination, or other unsafe or school-inappropriate conduct.

Matchmake may remove, restrict, or preserve content where needed to enforce these Terms, protect users, support schools, investigate reports, or comply with law.

## Match Reviews And Screenshot Extraction

Matchmake may allow users to manually enter match review data or upload scoreboard screenshots for extraction. Users are responsible for reviewing extracted data before saving or relying on it.

The screenshot extraction feature uses automated processing and may produce incomplete or incorrect results. It is a convenience feature, not an official scoring authority.

Users may upload only screenshots they are authorized to submit and that are relevant to team match review. Users should avoid uploading unrelated personal information, private messages, student IDs, disciplinary records, or other sensitive material.

## User Content

The user or school retains ownership of User Content. By submitting User Content, the user and, where applicable, the school grant Matchmake a limited license to host, process, display, transmit, secure, support, and otherwise use that content only as needed to provide and improve the Matchmake service, comply with school instructions, enforce these Terms, protect the service, and comply with law.

Users represent that they have the rights and permissions needed to submit User Content to Matchmake.

## Privacy And Student Data

Matchmake handles personal information as described in the Matchmake School-Facing Privacy Policy Draft or the final published privacy policy once adopted.

Where Matchmake receives or maintains student information on behalf of a school, Matchmake will use that information for school-authorized service purposes and not for unrelated commercial profiling or targeted advertising.

Matchmake will not sell student data, rent or trade student data, disclose student data to advertisers, ad networks, or data brokers, use student data for targeted advertising or behavioral advertising, or use student data to market products or services to students, parents/guardians, or households.

Service providers and subprocessors may use student data only as needed to provide, secure, maintain, process, or support Matchmake, and may not use student data for their own advertising, marketing, sale, data-broker, model-training, or unrelated commercial purposes unless a school expressly authorizes a different use in writing.

The student-data commitments are documented in `docs/student-data-no-sale-no-ads.md`.

Schools remain responsible for their own FERPA notices, directory information designations, consent processes, and local student privacy obligations. Matchmake will reasonably support school requests to access, correct, export, or delete school-controlled information, subject to technical limits, legal requirements, and the separate data deletion/export process to be finalized.
