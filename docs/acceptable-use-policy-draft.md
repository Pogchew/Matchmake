# Matchmake Acceptable Use Policy Draft

Status: Draft for school/district and legal review

Last updated: 2026-06-18

This draft is written for students, coaches, school esports administrators, and teams using Matchmake. Replace bracketed placeholders and review with school/district leadership and counsel before publication.

## Purpose

Matchmake is for school-authorized esports team operations. The goal is to help teams find scrims, coordinate schedules, communicate with active opponents, and review match performance in a safe, respectful, school-appropriate environment.

This Acceptable Use Policy explains what behavior is expected and what is not allowed.

Matchmake's school launch documents say student data may not be sold, used for targeted advertising, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used to build unrelated commercial profiles. Those commitments are documented in `docs/student-data-no-sale-no-ads.md`.

## Who Must Follow This Policy

This policy applies to:

- Students and players.
- Coaches, teachers, club sponsors, and school staff.
- Organization owners and team administrators.
- Anyone using a Matchmake account, scrim listing, chat, roster, calendar link, match review, screenshot extraction, or other Matchmake feature.

If your school, district, league, tournament, or state association has stricter rules, those stricter rules also apply.

For the MOSEF / high-school pilot, children under 13 may not use Matchmake unless their school or district has specifically authorized that use and the required COPPA notice and consent path described in `docs/under-13-coppa-handling.md` is complete.

## Core Expectations

Use Matchmake in a way that is:

- Respectful.
- Honest.
- School-appropriate.
- Safe for students and staff.
- Limited to legitimate esports team coordination and match review.
- Consistent with your school or district code of conduct.

## Account And Access Rules

Users must:

- Use only accounts they are authorized to use.
- Keep passwords and login links private.
- Use accurate names, display names, team names, and organization information.
- Tell a coach, school administrator, or `[support contact email]` if they believe an account has been accessed without permission.

Users may not:

- Share accounts without school or Matchmake approval.
- Impersonate another person, school, district, team, or tournament official.
- Use another person's account.
- Try to bypass authentication, row-level access controls, rate limits, or other security controls.
- Probe, scrape, overload, disrupt, reverse engineer, or attack Matchmake.

## Team, Roster, And Profile Rules

Team and roster information should be accurate, school-approved, and appropriate for the audience where it appears.

Users may not:

- Add a student/player to a roster without authorization.
- Publish private student information without authorization.
- Add profile links that expose unsafe, inappropriate, or unrelated personal information.
- Use offensive, discriminatory, sexual, threatening, or harassing names, team names, roster entries, profile text, links, or logos.
- Falsify team rank, region, roster, school identity, no-show history, or other competitive information.

Schools should decide whether player names, gamer tags, profile links, and participation in esports may be displayed publicly under their own policies and FERPA/directory-information notices.

The current product expectations and review checklist for optional links are documented in `docs/profile-link-safety-guidance.md`.

## Scrim Posting And Request Rules

Scrim listings and requests must be made in good faith.

Users may not:

- Post fake scrims or misleading availability.
- Request scrims for teams they do not represent.
- Spam teams with repeated or unwanted requests.
- Use scrim requests to harass, pressure, shame, or target another team.
- Cancel, decline, or manipulate scrims in bad faith.
- Coordinate cheating, match fixing, gambling, or prohibited competitive conduct.
- Misrepresent a match as official, sanctioned, or school-approved when it is not.

Teams should show up on time, communicate schedule changes early, and use the app to keep scrim status accurate.

## Chat And Communication Rules

Scrim chat is for active scrim coordination. Keep messages focused on scheduling, lobby details, match setup, follow-up, and sportsmanship.

Users may not send or share:

- Harassment, bullying, cyberbullying, threats, intimidation, or retaliation.
- Hate speech, discriminatory slurs, or attacks based on race, color, national origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, or any other protected or school-protected status.
- Sexual content, grooming behavior, exploitative content, or requests for sexual images or contact.
- Doxxing or private personal information, including home addresses, phone numbers, student IDs, private emails, passwords, private social accounts, or family information.
- Self-harm encouragement, violent threats, or instructions for harm.
- Cheating tools, exploits, malware, phishing links, token theft attempts, or suspicious downloads.
- Spam, scams, advertising, or unrelated promotions.
- Attempts to move school-supervised student communication into unsafe or unauthorized channels.

StopBullying.gov describes cyberbullying as harmful, false, mean, or private content shared through digital devices or online spaces, including gaming. Matchmake treats that conduct as a serious violation.

## Match Review And Screenshot Rules

Match reviews and screenshot extraction are for team performance review.

Users may not:

- Upload screenshots they are not authorized to use.
- Upload private messages, student IDs, disciplinary records, medical information, or unrelated personal information.
- Save extracted data without reviewing it for obvious errors.
- Alter match data to shame, harass, or mislead another student/team.
- Use match review notes for bullying, insults, discriminatory comments, or retaliation.

Automated extraction can be wrong. Coaches/admins should review extracted player rows, stats, scores, maps, and dates before using them for team decisions.

## Public Scrim Board And Calendar Link Rules

Some limited scrim listing information may be public so teams can find opponents.

Users may not:

- Put private student information into public scrim listing fields.
- Use public listings to target, mock, or harass other teams.
- Share private calendar subscription links outside the intended school/team audience.
- Use calendar links to track, expose, or pressure students or teams outside legitimate team coordination.

Organization owners should rotate or disable calendar links if they are shared too broadly.

## Safety, Reporting, And Escalation

If you see unsafe behavior, preserve what you can and report it.

Students should:

- Stop engaging if a conversation becomes unsafe.
- Save screenshots or details when possible.
- Tell a coach, parent/guardian, trusted adult, or school administrator.
- Use any school/district reporting process.

Coaches/admins should:

- Review reports promptly.
- Preserve relevant Matchmake records when needed.
- Remove or restrict users, teams, scrims, chat, or match review content when appropriate.
- Escalate emergencies, threats, harassment, discrimination, or student-safety concerns through school/district procedures.
- Contact Matchmake at `[support contact email]` for platform support.

The pilot reporting workflow, severity levels, evidence handling, response targets, and school/Matchmake responsibilities are defined in `docs/inappropriate-behavior-reporting-escalation.md`. Urgent safety issues must go through emergency and school safety processes first; the Matchmake support inbox is not an emergency service.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and school instructions, violations may result in:

- Warning or coaching intervention.
- Content removal.
- Loss of chat, scrim, team, or organization privileges.
- Account suspension or removal.
- Team or organization restriction.
- School/district discipline.
- League or tournament action.
- Referral to school safety staff or law enforcement when appropriate.

Matchmake may preserve records when needed to investigate misuse, support a school, protect users, comply with law, or enforce Terms.

## Coach And Administrator Responsibilities

Coaches and organization owners should:

- Set expectations before students use Matchmake.
- Keep rosters accurate and authorized.
- Review public team and scrim listing fields.
- Monitor active scrim behavior according to school policy.
- Respond to reports consistently.
- Remove access when a user leaves the team or should no longer use the service.
- Confirm that any external profile links are school-appropriate.

## Student Responsibilities

Students should:

- Treat opponents, teammates, coaches, and admins with respect.
- Keep personal information private.
- Ask a coach before adding roster/profile information they are unsure about.
- Report bullying, harassment, threats, doxxing, cheating, or unsafe behavior.
- Review match data honestly.
- Follow school, team, tournament, and platform rules.
