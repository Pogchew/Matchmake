# Matchmake Student Data No-Sale And No-Advertising Commitments

Status: Defined launch language for school/district and legal review

Last updated: 2026-06-18

This document defines the clear no-sale, no-advertising, and no-commercial-profiling commitments Matchmake should use for the MOSEF / high-school launch documents. It is written to align the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and School/District Data Privacy Agreement around one plain-language position.

## Core Commitment

Matchmake will use student data only to provide, secure, support, maintain, and improve the school-authorized Matchmake service.

Matchmake will not:

- Sell student data.
- Rent, trade, license, or otherwise disclose student data to advertisers, ad networks, data brokers, or similar third parties.
- Use student data for targeted advertising, behavioral advertising, or retargeting.
- Use student data or student metadata to build advertising, marketing, or unrelated commercial profiles.
- Use student data to market or advertise products or services to students, parents/guardians, or households.
- Allow service providers or subprocessors to use student data for their own advertising, marketing, sale, data-broker, or unrelated commercial purposes.

These limits apply to student account information, roster information, gamer tags, profile fields, scrim listings and requests, scrim chat, match reviews, screenshot extraction inputs and outputs, usage metadata, support records, and school-controlled records.

## Allowed Service Uses

Matchmake may use student data only for service-related purposes, including:

- Creating and managing accounts, organizations, teams, rosters, scrims, match reviews, and optional calendar feeds.
- Providing active scrim chat and match review workflows.
- Processing optional scoreboard screenshots to return structured match data.
- Securing, debugging, rate-limiting, monitoring, and maintaining the service.
- Supporting school-authorized access, export, correction, deletion, moderation, safety, and privacy requests.
- Producing de-identified or aggregated operational metrics only when they cannot reasonably identify a student, household, school-controlled record, or small group in context.

## Service Providers And Integrations

Matchmake may share student data with service providers only when needed to provide, secure, maintain, process, or support the Matchmake service.

Service providers and subprocessors should be bound to use student data only for the services they provide to Matchmake and should not use student data for their own advertising, marketing, sale, data-broker, model-training, or unrelated commercial purposes unless the school expressly authorizes a different use in writing.

For launch, expected service providers and integrations should remain listed in the Privacy Policy and Data Privacy Agreement.

## De-Identified And Aggregated Data

De-identified or aggregated analytics may be used only to operate, secure, understand, and improve Matchmake when the data cannot reasonably identify a student, household, school-controlled record, or small group in context.

Matchmake should not attempt to re-identify de-identified data and should require authorized recipients of de-identified data to agree not to re-identify it.

De-identified or aggregated data should not be used to target advertising or marketing to students, parents/guardians, households, schools, or school communities.

## Publication Checklist

Before publication or district signature:

- Confirm the legal entity, privacy contact, support contact, and subprocessor list.
- Confirm that the Privacy Policy, Terms, DPA, and any order form do not contain conflicting advertising, marketing, analytics, model-training, or license language.
- Confirm whether any de-identified or aggregated analytics are permitted by the participating school or district.
- Confirm whether state student privacy laws or district procurement terms require stronger wording.
- Confirm counsel has reviewed the final student-data use commitments.
