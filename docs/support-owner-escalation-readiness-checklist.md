Status: Blocked final launch gate checklist

This checklist summarizes the remaining named-owner and escalation decisions before Matchmake can confirm the MOSEF support owner and escalation path for student launch. It does not replace the support process, behavior escalation runbook, school safety procedures, or legal review.

## Current Coverage

The operating model is documented:

- Routine support uses one published pilot support inbox.
- Students start with coach, school esports lead, parent/guardian, trusted adult, or school reporting channel.
- Support requests are tracked in a restricted support log.
- Safety, privacy, admin-removal, monitoring, and product-feedback issues route to separate workflows.
- Behavior reports use a school-first path plus Matchmake platform escalation.
- The support inbox is not an emergency service and must not be presented as continuously monitored.
- Reports must be tracked in a restricted case log outside the app until a dedicated queue exists.
- Current product limitations are explicit: no student-facing report form, no dedicated case database, and owner dashboard activity logs are not a complete evidence archive.

## Required Decisions Before Confirmation

### Matchmake Owners

- Name the primary Matchmake support process owner.
- Name the backup support process owner.
- Name the primary Matchmake incident response owner.
- Name the backup incident response owner.
- Name the production/logging escalation owner for repeated runtime errors.
- Confirm who can approve admin-removal actions during the pilot.

### Contacts And Hours

- Publish the final support inbox.
- Publish the final reporting contact.
- Publish the pilot escalation contact for MOSEF coordinators.
- Define staffed business hours.
- Define backup coverage outside the primary owner's availability.
- Define expected response windows for Priority 0, Priority 1, Priority 2, and Priority 3 support.
- Confirm launch materials point coaches/admins to the same published support path.

### Logs And Access Control

- Select the restricted support log location.
- Define the restricted support log access list.
- Confirm support records include support ID, received time, school, requester role, summary, priority, owner, status, and next update date.
- Select the restricted case-log location for safety or behavior reports.
- Define the restricted case-log access list.
- Confirm case records include case ID, received time, source, severity, incident owner, school contact, access restrictions, actions, and closure outcome.
- Confirm support records and case logs are separate from public issue trackers, broad Discord channels, student group chats, and ordinary product planning notes.

### School Contacts

- Collect a primary school safety or esports contact for every pilot school.
- Collect a backup school safety or esports contact for every pilot school.
- Confirm each school knows its emergency, student-safety, Title IX, civil-rights, and discipline processes remain school-owned.
- Confirm each school knows when to request Matchmake platform action.
- Confirm MOSEF coordinator escalation expectations.

### Safety And Escalation

- Confirm Severity 0 emergency language with participating schools.
- Confirm whether counsel must review suspected child sexual exploitation handling.
- Confirm legal and mandated-reporting ownership.
- Confirm evidence preservation rules and access restrictions.
- Confirm evidence and case retention periods.
- Prepare acknowledgement, school-handoff, status-update, and closure templates.
- Test one tabletop scenario for each severity before student launch.

## Ready Criteria

The final launch gate can be checked off when:

- All Matchmake support and incident owners are named.
- The support inbox, reporting contact, pilot escalation contact, and business hours are published in launch materials.
- Restricted support-log and case-log locations exist with approved access lists.
- Every pilot school has supplied primary and backup school/MOSEF contacts.
- Operators know when to route to emergency, school safety, privacy, support, admin removal, monitoring, or product feedback.
- Any missing owners, contacts, logs, or school contacts are explicitly accepted as launch exceptions with owner, date, scope, and follow-up requirement.
