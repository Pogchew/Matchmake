# Matchmake First-Scrim Posting Guide

Status: MOSEF / high-school pilot guide
Audience: coaches, esports leads, and school administrators posting a first scrim
Prerequisite: at least one organization team exists in Matchmake

This guide covers the first open scrim listing. Use `docs/first-team-setup-guide.md` first if the school has not created a team yet.

## Before You Post

Confirm these details before opening the Post Scrim form:

- Which team is posting the scrim.
- Date and start time in the coach/admin's local time zone.
- Expected number of games or maps.
- Team location or region.
- Acceptable opponent rank range.
- Any rules, map pool, lobby, communication, or eligibility notes opponents need before requesting.

Do not include student phone numbers, private handles, personal emails, meeting passwords, or sensitive school information in the public listing notes.

## Post The Listing

1. Sign in with the coach/admin account.
2. Open **Find Scrims**.
3. Select **Post Scrim** or open `/?post=true`.
4. Choose the **Posting team**.
   - If no teams are available, create a team first at `/team/new`.
   - The **Game** field is locked to the selected team's game.
5. Set the **Date** and **Start time**.
   - The form uses the current user's local time zone.
   - Pick a time the coach can supervise or has approved.
6. Select the expected **Games** count.
   - Use this to communicate match length, such as one game, best of three, or a longer block.
7. Confirm **Team location**.
   - This should match the region that gives realistic scheduling and ping expectations.
8. Set the **Opponent rank range**.
   - Start broad for the first listing unless the team needs a narrow competitive band.
   - Avoid using rank as a gate for students outside the school's approved competitive policy.
9. Add **Notes for opponents**.
   - Good notes include map pool, format, lobby expectations, rule constraints, or preferred communication timing.
   - Keep notes short and school-appropriate.
10. Select **Post Listing**.

After posting, the listing appears on the scrim board as `open` until another team requests it or the scheduled time passes.

## Verify The Listing

After the confirmation:

- Confirm the listing appears on **Find Scrims**.
- Check the team, game, rank range, location, date, time, and game count.
- Confirm it is not accidentally posted under the wrong team.
- Open `/calendar` and check that the listing appears as an open listing on the selected date.
- If the listing is wrong and still open, open the scrim detail page and edit or retract your own listing rather than relying on chat to correct a bad listing.

## When Another Team Requests The Scrim

Use `/requests` to manage responses.

- **Needs Reply** means another team requested your listing.
- **Waiting** means your team requested someone else's listing and is waiting for a response.
- **Confirmed** means both sides are matched.

For a first scrim, coaches should review the opposing team name, school/organization, game, rank range, region, and scheduled time before accepting. Decline requests that do not fit the school schedule, opponent expectations, or conduct requirements.

## Chat And Coordination

Once a request is active or confirmed, use scrim chat only for practical coordination:

- Confirm lobby or room details.
- Confirm map, mode, format, or side-selection process.
- Confirm timing changes.
- Ask clarifying scheduling questions.

Do not use Matchmake chat for trash talk, student discipline, private contact exchange, sensitive student details, or emergency reporting. Use the school's normal reporting path and `docs/inappropriate-behavior-reporting-escalation.md` for safety or conduct concerns.

## Calendar Check

Use `/calendar` after posting or accepting:

- `Open Listing` means your listing is still available.
- `Pending` means a request is waiting on one side.
- `Confirmed` means the scrim is scheduled with an opponent.

Before practice starts, confirm the calendar status, opponent, game, time, and date. If the Discord/calendar integration panel is not configured for the school, use the in-app calendar and any school-approved calendar process instead.

## After The Scrim

After the match:

1. Mark the scrim played from the request workflow when appropriate.
2. Save the match review from the team dashboard.
3. Confirm the saved review is attached to the correct team, game, opponent, date, and scrim when available.
4. Keep the source screenshot or result record until the saved review looks correct.

Use the match review upload guide when it is added.

## Common First-Post Mistakes

- **No team exists.** Create the team first before posting.
- **Wrong posting team.** Re-check the team selector when an organization has multiple teams.
- **Wrong time zone assumption.** The form displays the user's local time zone; coordinate carefully across regions.
- **Rank window too narrow.** A narrow range can make first matches harder to find.
- **Notes include private information.** Keep public listing notes practical and non-sensitive.
- **Forgetting requests.** Posting is not the finish line; check `/requests` for inbound teams.
- **Treating chat as a report channel.** Safety and conduct reports should follow the school's reporting process.

## Ready For First Match

The scrim is ready when:

- The listing details are accurate on Find Scrims and Calendar.
- The coach/admin knows where to check Needs Reply, Waiting, and Confirmed requests.
- Both teams have agreed to the schedule and format.
- Communication stays in school-appropriate channels.
- The coach/admin knows how the result will be saved after the match.
